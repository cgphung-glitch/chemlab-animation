Purpose & Objective

Determine Precision & Accuracy of Lab Volume Devices

In quantitative chemistry, measurements form the backbone of all data analysis. This experiment investigates how well different laboratory glassware—specifically a graduated beaker, a graduated cylinder, and a 10-mL pipet—can measure exactly 10 mL of water by determining mass and utilizing temperature-dependent density values.

Mistakes vs. Errors

Distinguish between avoidable blunders (mistakes) and inevitable measurement variations (systematic and random errors).
