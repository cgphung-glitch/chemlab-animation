Naming the lines: α, β, γ …

Within a series, individual lines are traditionally labeled with Greek letters in order of increasing transition energy, starting from the line closest to the series limit's low-energy side: n₁+1 → n₁ is alpha (α), n₁+2 → n₁ is beta (β), n₁+3 → n₁ is gamma (γ), then delta (δ), epsilon (ε), zeta (ζ), and so on. As n₂ increases, successive lines crowd closer together and converge on the series limit as n₂ → ∞.
