Ideal gas: PV = nRT. Real gas: van der Waals (P + a·n²/V²)(V − n·b) = nRT, solved numerically where needed. R = 8.314 L·kPa·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹. Van der Waals constants are literature values converted to kPa·L²·mol⁻² and L·mol⁻¹. The container visual is capped at 10–200 mL — values computed outside this range are still shown numerically but the piston is drawn pinned at its physical limit. Near a gas's critical point the van der Waals equation can have more than one mathematical solution for volume or moles; the solver returns one physically reasonable root and may not reflect phase-transition behavior. Particle count and speed are illustrative, not literal molecule counts or velocities.
