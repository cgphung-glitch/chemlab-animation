Dot density is proportional to the actual quantum-mechanical probability |ψ|² (random-sampled "electron cloud" — the same style used in most textbooks), not a smoothed isosurface. Color shows the sign (phase) of the real wavefunction. mₗ selects the standard real orbital (e.g. l=1: mₗ=0 → pz, ±1 → px/py) rather than the complex e^{imφ} form, matching how these are drawn in general chemistry.

Average surface replaces the dots with smooth, translucent surfaces of constant |ψ|², chosen so that the surface encloses the selected percentage (default 90 %) of the electron probability. Orange and blue lobes again mark ψ > 0 and ψ < 0.
